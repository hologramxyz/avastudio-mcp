You see the frames of a short launch film, one every 0.5 s. File fNNN.jpg is at (NNN - 1) x 0.5 s. You know nothing else about the film. Do not guess what it is meant to show; write only what you see.

For each frame, one line:
- the time, from the file name
- what fills the frame: a product's UI in use, a product's finished output (a video, an image, a document), a photo of a product, a title, a logo, a number, a card, a wall of tiles, or blank
- how much of the frame the main thing fills, as a rough %
- whether any shown output is finished work or a loading, progress or empty state
- any text you cannot read at phone size, any text or card cut by the frame edge, and any picture that looks soft or blown up

Then answer, citing frame files:
1. Which seconds show a product in use or its finished output, large enough to see what it is?
2. What is on screen at 0-3 s? Would a stranger know what the product is by then?
3. What is the strongest frame, and what is the weakest?
4. What does the film sell, in one sentence, from the frames alone?
