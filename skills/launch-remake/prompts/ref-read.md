You are a senior motion designer reverse-engineering a launch film so another designer can rebuild its motion, depth and taste exactly on a different product. Be concrete and measurable. Sent at 10 fps. Give ranges, not adjectives.

1. SHOT TABLE: every shot and every transition with start and end in mm:ss.s, what is on screen, how it enters, how it exits. Say which moves are one continuous camera move and where a dissolve or cut truly happens.
2. CAMERA: for each shot, the apparent camera: tilt direction, approx rotateX, rotateY, rotateZ in degrees, perspective strength (weak, medium, strong), the direction and rough speed of travel (percent of frame per second), and the ease of each move (linear drift, fast start then long settle, S curve).
3. DEPTH: what makes it read as deep. Focus falloff: where the sharp band sits, how fast blur grows, approx blur at the far edge as percent of frame width. Vignette: size, softness, how dark. Fog or fade into black. Shadows, rim light, edge highlights on the panels.
4. LIGHT AND COLOUR: background hex, panel hex, text hex at full and at dim, accent hexes. Any light that moves across the surface, and how.
5. UI ACTING: how each UI element comes alive. Type-on rate, caret, streaming, staggered rows, opacity ramps, status icons, how a panel opens. Durations and staggers in seconds.
6. TYPE AND LOGO: how the end line and logo arrive and leave: per letter or word, blur, gradient sweep, timing.
7. PACING: per section, seconds, and how long the eye gets to read before the next thing starts.
8. AUDIO: if there is sound, say what you hear, section by section. Say plainly if you are unsure.
9. SIGNATURE: the 7 things that most make this film look like itself, specific enough to copy.
10. TRAPS: what a lazy copy would get wrong, and what would make it look fake.
